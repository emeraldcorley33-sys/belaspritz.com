Do Not Sell My Personal Information

Last updated: November 25, 2025

Welcome — your privacy matters to us. We don’t sell your personal information, and we strive to keep things simple and transparent.

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We aim to honor those same principles for everyone.

Your Rights

  • Request disclosure of what personal information we collect
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of any future sale or sharing of your data

How to Submit a Request

To exercise your rights, please email us at sip@belaspritz.com or use our Contact Form. We will respond within 45 days as required by law.

Authorized Agents

You may designate an authorized agent to submit requests on your behalf. We may require verification of your identity and authorization.

Also see our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
